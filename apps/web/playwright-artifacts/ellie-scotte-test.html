About Ellie Scotte

Uyiosa Elyeke Eworo, known as Ellie Scotte, is a Nigerian gospel singer and songwriter from Cross River State. She's built a name for herself with a voice that carries real depth and a sound that blends gospel, R&B, and African gospel influences into something honest and moving.

Ellie's music has always been about more than melody. Songs like "Behold You," "Every Step," "Need You More," and her single "Eleazar" all come from a place of real surrender. Each one plays like a prayer, written for anyone carrying a weight they can't put down, pointing them back to God as their strength and their peace.

She's teamed up with fellow Energize Music artists on records like "Ateriba," alongside Greatman Takit, Lex Ash, and Excel Joab, and "He's Doing More" with Excel Joab. Whether she's on a solo record or a feature, Ellie brings the same thing every time: a voice that's Spirit-led, and a heart set on leading people closer to God.
